About PawConnect

We are a civic-tech platform connecting shelter animals with compatible humans — using AI to increase adoption success and reduce animal abandonment in the Philippines.

🌱 Our Mission

PawConnect was built around a simple belief: the right match between human and animal changes both lives. Too many adoptions fail because they are based on appearance alone. Our AI-powered compatibility engine bridges this gap — analyzing lifestyle, space, activity level, and household dynamics against each animal's behavioral profile, creating matches that last.

🎯 SDG Alignment
SDG 3 — Good Health & Wellbeing
Companion animals are clinically proven to reduce anxiety, depression and loneliness. Every adoption we facilitate is a mental health intervention.
SDG 15 — Life on Land
By promoting adoption over buying, we reduce stray populations, support ethical animal welfare, and discourage the illegal wildlife trade.

📋 Terms & Conditions
🐾 Responsible Pet Ownership

By using PawConnect, you commit to providing adopted animals with proper food, water, shelter, veterinary care, and affection. You agree not to rehome, abandon, or neglect any animal adopted through our platform without consulting a PawConnect-affiliated shelter.

💚 Adoption Ethics

PawConnect is a platform for ethical adoption only. Users agree not to use the platform to acquire animals for resale, breeding for profit, or any purpose other than providing a loving permanent home.

🔒 Privacy Notice

PawConnect collects only the information necessary to facilitate pet adoption. Your data is stored locally on your device for this demo and is never shared with third parties.

✅ User Responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of information you provide. Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Any reports of animal abuse or neglect will be forwarded to the appropriate authorities.

🌍 SDG Alignment

PawConnect is committed to UN SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) and SDG 15 (Life on Land). We expect all platform users to support these values through responsible, compassionate engagement with animals.
